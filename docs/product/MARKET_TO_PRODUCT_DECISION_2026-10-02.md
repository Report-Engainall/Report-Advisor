## Product implication

The generic opportunity “AI explains my dashboard” is already crowded.

Report-Advisor should therefore concentrate on the harder, more defensible chain:

`REAL SOURCE → CANONICAL TRUTH → EVIDENCE PASSPORT → EXPLAINED CLAIM → BUSINESS DECISION → APPROVAL → WORK → OBSERVED OUTCOME → LEARNING`

### Differentiation pillars

1. **Source-bound truth**
Every material result retains source hash, import job, tenant and evidence snapshot.

2. **Explainability with limits**
Distinguish observed, derived, inferred and recommended claims. Never turn correlation into causality.

3. **Business action**
A report finding can become a decision, approval, work item and measured outcome without losing provenance.

4. **Evidence-preserving history**
Historical output remains reproducible against its profile/version/evidence identity.

5. **Adaptive reports**
Known report shapes inherit versioned archetype intelligence; unknown reports receive a useful Generic Smart Pack rather than NO-OP.

6. **Arabic/SMB fit**
The product can turn messy Excel/PDF business sources, Arabic headers, branch/warehouse/customer realities and operational workflows into a governed decision surface.

## Product decision

Prioritize capabilities that strengthen:
`TRUST + ACTIONABILITY + TRACEABILITY + OUTCOME`

Do not spend the next product wave copying generic chatbot/report-summary features unless a concrete user journey requires them.

## Next measurable product outcomes

- Time-to-understanding from raw report to executive brief.
- Time-to-decision from insight to approved decision.
- Evidence traceability from statement to source.
- Operational completion from decision to work.
- Outcome readback rate.
- Percentage of claims with explicit provenance and limitation state.
