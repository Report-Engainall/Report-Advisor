## Observed market patterns

### Power BI
Microsoft documents report summaries and report Q&A grounded in report visuals, with citations/references to the visuals used. It also supports custom prompts and fact-based questions over report content.
Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/explore-reports/copilot-pane-summarize-content

### Tableau Pulse
Tableau documents insight answers backed by citations, with analysis of contributors, detractors, drivers, outliers, trends and concentrated contribution patterns.
Source: https://help.tableau.com/current/online/en-gb/pulse_ask_discover_qa.htm
Release notes: https://help.tableau.com/current/online/en-gb/pulse_intro.htm

### Oracle Analytics
Oracle Analytics Explain generates basic facts, key drivers, segments and anomalies; current documentation describes Explain as a statistical/ML insight capability over a target column.
Source: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/paas/analytics-cloud/tutorial-explain-multi-table/

### Looker
Google documents Conversational Analytics as natural-language analysis grounded in the Looker semantic model, with governed metric definitions as a source of truth.
Source: https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/conversational-analytics-overview

## Product implication

The generic space of “AI summary/chat with data” is already established. Report-Advisor should therefore concentrate on the harder end-to-end chain:

`REAL SOURCE → CANONICAL TRUTH → EVIDENCE PASSPORT → EXPLAINED CLAIM → BUSINESS DECISION → APPROVAL → WORK → OBSERVED OUTCOME → LEARNING`

### Differentiation pillars

1. **Source-bound truth** — every material result retains source hash, import job, tenant and evidence snapshot.
2. **Explainability with limits** — distinguish observed, derived, inferred and recommended claims; correlation is never promoted to causality.
3. **Business action** — findings can become decisions, approvals and work while retaining provenance.
4. **Evidence-preserving history** — historical output remains reproducible against profile/version/evidence identity.
5. **Adaptive reports** — known report shapes inherit versioned archetype intelligence; unknown reports receive a useful Generic Smart Pack rather than NO-OP.
6. **Arabic/SMB fit** — messy Excel/PDF sources, Arabic headers, branch/warehouse/customer dimensions and operational workflows become a governed decision surface.

## Product decision

Prioritize:
`TRUST + ACTIONABILITY + TRACEABILITY + OUTCOME`

Do not spend the next wave copying generic chatbot/report-summary behavior unless a concrete business journey requires it.

## Measurable outcomes

- Time-to-understanding from raw report to executive brief.
- Time-to-decision from insight to approved decision.
- Evidence traceability from statement to source.
- Operational completion from decision to work.
- Outcome readback rate.
- Percentage of material claims with explicit provenance and limitation state.
